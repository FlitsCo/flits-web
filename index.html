How the lab runs

The long version

01

Classes, not instances

A person approves a template, a tone, a range a price may move within. Agents produce the instances inside those bounds.

02

Checks, not instructions

Where an output can be verified by a program, the program is the gate. What reaches a person is the part that needs taste.

03

Irreversible stays human

Money leaving, anything signed, anything said to a stranger under the Flits name. Those are not bottlenecks to engineer away.
